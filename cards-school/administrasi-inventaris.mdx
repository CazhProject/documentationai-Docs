title: 'Administrasi: Inventaris'
description: >-
  Panduan mengelola inventaris barang di CARDS School — melihat, menambah,
  mengubah, dan menghapus data barang serta riwayat inventaris.
---
Menu Inventaris digunakan admin sekolah untuk mencatat dan memantau seluruh barang milik instansi beserta riwayat pergerakannya. Terdapat dua submenu utama: **Barang** dan **Riwayat**, diakses melalui menu **Administrasi** → submenu **Inventaris**.

1. Login ke website, kemudian klik menu **Administrasi**
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-9f0069f2-e3f7-48a0-be6a-ee3f46a1d606/doc-0793c7e9-51db-4bf5-85cf-91406e00f125/1784879957712-qavuj8foqm-pasted-image-1784879949870.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1870" height="990" alt="" />
2. Kemudian klik submenu **Inventory**
   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-9f0069f2-e3f7-48a0-be6a-ee3f46a1d606/doc-0793c7e9-51db-4bf5-85cf-91406e00f125/1784880021244-3eszxpx5soc-pasted-image-1784880020240.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1882" height="1000" alt="" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-9f0069f2-e3f7-48a0-be6a-ee3f46a1d606/doc-0793c7e9-51db-4bf5-85cf-91406e00f125/1784879145220-lfq9hnuwyv-pasted-image-1784879143893.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1876" height="1000" alt="" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-9f0069f2-e3f7-48a0-be6a-ee3f46a1d606/doc-0793c7e9-51db-4bf5-85cf-91406e00f125/1784880602776-8uotk0izc3-pasted-image-1784880601392.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1890" height="992" alt="" />
